# Statistics

Open **Settings > General > Statistics** to configure the compact statistics on Home.

**Show Statistics** shows or hides the Home statistics panel. The same switch is available under **Quick Show/Hide**.

**Time In Range** selects the range calculation used by the Home panel. The detailed Statistics tab and PDF reports use standard clinical ranges.

**HbA1c in mmols/mol** shows the estimated HbA1c using the IFCC unit.

The Home period menu includes today, 1 day, 7 days, 30 days, and 90 days. Statistics are only as complete as the stored readings.

Open the **Statistics** tab for detailed CGM statistics, trend charts, daily summaries, and PDF report generation. See [Statistics](statistics.md).
