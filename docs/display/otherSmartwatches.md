# Other smartwatches

xDrip4iOS has no direct companion app for Fitbit, Garmin, Xiaomi, or Amazfit devices.

Some third-party watch faces and apps can read data from Nightscout. In that arrangement:

1. xDrip4iOS or another uploader sends readings to Nightscout.
2. The third-party watch app downloads readings from Nightscout.
3. The watch vendor and third-party app control update timing.

Follow the instructions supplied by the watch app. Do not enter your main Nightscout API secret when a restricted access token can provide the required read access.

Third-party smartwatch software is not part of xDrip4iOS. Its compatibility can change independently.
